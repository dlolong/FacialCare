# Branch and Service Research Seed Data

The branch and service records in this website were gathered from publicly discoverable internet records and supplied company marketing materials. They are included to demonstrate how a nationwide, multi-branch website could work.

This is **research seed data**, not an official company master list.

## Important limitations

- Duplicate, incomplete and conflicting branch records may exist online.
- Calamba is represented as one customer-facing location until Head Office confirms whether the headquarters and facial clinic are separate public locations.
- Addresses, contacts and schedules require Head Office confirmation.
- Services and promotions may vary by branch.
- No branch-to-service mappings have been approved.
- Exact pricing must come from Head Office before publication.
- Entries marked as needing owner confirmation remain excluded from customer-facing directories and booking.
- Demo images remain subject to company approval before public launch.
